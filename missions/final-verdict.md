# Final — Architecture Verdict

## Briefing
A fictional organization needs one platform for three workloads: long multi-GPU training jobs, interactive model development, and always-on inference services. Teams are lobbying for their favorite tool.

## Challenge
Write an architecture recommendation that may select one system, a combination, or different systems for different workload classes.

## Required evidence
- workload requirements table
- evidence from earlier rounds
- scheduling/failure/control-path diagrams
- operational burden analysis
- GPU allocation considerations
- migration/complexity risks

## Constraint changes
After drafting the recommendation, add two surprises: cluster grows 10×; and multiple teams require fair resource sharing. Re-evaluate the verdict.

## Victory condition
Your conclusion is traceable to experiments and workload requirements, not “tool X is industry standard.”

The Colosseum is complete when you can defend the losers as intelligently as the winner.
